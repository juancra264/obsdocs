
## ESP32-C5-WIFI6-KIT-N16R8

IoT scenarios such as smart gateways and multi-protocol device integration.

  
Designed by [Waveshare](https://www.waveshare.com/esp32-c5-wifi6-kit-n16r4.htm)

 Module: ESP32-C5-WROOM-1
 
- CPU: 240MHz (Single-core RISC-V chip)
- Static Ram: 384KB
- PSRAM: 8MB
- Flash: 16MB Flash  

Features:

- Dual-band Wi-Fi 6 (2.4GHz and 5GHz)
- BLE 5
- Zigbee
- Thread
- Onboard batt. Recharge management module, with reserved 3.7V MX1.25 Lithium batt. header for external batt.
- USB Type-C port.
- Multiple low-power operating modes
